# Extras cheats

The complete-current Wii U build restores the four original FNaF 3 cheat options. The Cheats page unlocks after Night 6 and all six Good Ending minigames are complete.

- **Fast Nights**: advances the night clock at twice the normal rate.
- **Radar**: displays a white marker with a red border over Springtrap's real camera or vent source. It reports `OFFICE` when he has reached an office side.
- **Aggressive**: gives Springtrap a second AI update every gameplay frame, effectively making his movement and vent timers twice as aggressive.
- **No Errors**: prevents camera, audio and ventilation system failures while enabled. Phantom encounters remain active.

Cheats stay selected until the application is closed. They are not written to the save file.

Completing Night 6 with **Aggressive enabled and every other cheat disabled** saves the fourth title-screen star. The achievement uses the previously reserved byte 8 of the existing 16-byte version-1 save file, so older saves remain compatible.
