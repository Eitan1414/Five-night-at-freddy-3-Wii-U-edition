# Secret minigames on Wii U

The six Good Ending minigames remain available from Extras after Night 5. They can also be discovered during a night with controller-friendly equivalents of the original PC/console triggers.

## Hidden triggers

- **BB's Air Adventure**: open CAM 08, hold **L**, press **A**.
- **Mangle's Quest**: from Night 2 onward, open CAM 07, hold **X or Y**, then enter the four D-pad corners: upper-left, lower-left, upper-right, lower-right.
- **Chica's Party**: from Night 3 onward, hold **L** and press **A** once on each of CAM 02, CAM 03, CAM 04 and CAM 06.
- **Stage 01**: from Night 4 onward, pan to the left wall in the office. Hold **L** and press **A** for the keypad code `3-9-5-2-4-8`, using these D-pad positions: upper-right, lower-right, centre, up, left, down.
- **Shadow Bonnie**: from Night 5 onward, pan fully right in the office, hold **L**, press **A**.
- **Happiest Day**: open CAM 03, hold **R**, press **A**.

A hidden minigame pauses the active night. Leaving or completing it returns to the office. Progress is saved using the same version-1 save file as night completion.

## Minigame controls

- D-pad / left stick: move
- A: interact or change Shadow Bonnie room
- R: alternate Shadow Bonnie room control
- B: leave before completion

Happiest Day displays the Good Ending when all six saved minigame flags are complete. If Happiest Day was finished early, replay it after the other five.
